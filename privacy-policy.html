Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 8, 2026

Tour Diary Pro is an offline tool designed for Maharashtra Government Officials to maintain their daily tour diaries and technical reports.

1. Data Collection and Usage

100% Offline Operation: This application operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, location information, or diary entries to any external servers.

Local Storage: All information you enter is stored locally on your device's internal storage. This data is not accessible by the developer or any third parties.

2. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage/Media: To save and export generated PDF reports to your device.

3. Data Security

Since your data is stored only on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security settings. Please ensure you perform regular backups, as clearing your browser or app data may result in permanent loss of information.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties, as we do not have access to your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us via the official channels provided in the app.
